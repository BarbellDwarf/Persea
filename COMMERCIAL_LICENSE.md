## Overview

persea is available under a dual-license model:

1. **Open Source (AGPL-3.0)** — Free for self-hosted use, including commercial use, provided that all modifications are also released under AGPL-3.0. See [LICENSE](LICENSE) for the full text.

2. **Commercial License** — For organizations that need to modify persea without open-sourcing their changes. This is a paid license with terms negotiated per engagement.

persea also ships in two versions: a free, self-hosted edition that runs without a license key, and an **Enterprise** edition whose features (SAML SSO, fine-grained RBAC, TOTP/MFA enforcement, audit log retention and compliance exports, encrypted session recording, high availability / clustering) are unlocked by a commercial license key (`PSEA-<base64>`). See [docs/licensing.md](docs/licensing.md) for the two-version model, the 30-day evaluation period, and how to install a license key.

## When You Need a Commercial License

You need a Commercial License if:

- You want to modify persea for internal use without releasing your modifications as open source
- You distribute a modified persea and want to keep your modifications proprietary
- You offer persea as a managed service and want to avoid AGPL-3.0's network clause obligations
- Your organization's policies prohibit AGPL-licensed software

You do NOT need a Commercial License if:

- You use the unmodified persea for any purpose (including commercial use)
- You're willing to release your modifications under AGPL-3.0
- You're a homelab user or individual using persea for personal use
